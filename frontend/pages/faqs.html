Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Do you deliver all over India?

Yes, we provide shipping across India. Delivery times may vary depending on your location.

2. How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually delivered within 3–7 business days depending on your city and product availability.

3. Can I return or exchange a saree?

Yes, returns or exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery, provided the product is unused, unwashed, and in original condition.

4. How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking ID via email or SMS to check your order status.

5. What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery in selected areas.

6. Are the saree colors exactly the same as shown in images?

We try to display colors as accurately as possible, but slight variations may occur due to lighting and screen differences.

7. Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?

Yes, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for most locations in India.

8. How can I contact customer support?

You can contact us via email, contact form, or WhatsApp support if available.
